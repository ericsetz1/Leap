Booking & billing FAQ

Do I need a referral from my pediatrician?

[Edit for your state's direct-access laws.] Many families can start PT without a referral, but some insurance plans require a prescription for reimbursement. Contact us and we'll help you sort it out.

How do I pay for my visit?

Payments are processed securely through Jane. You can keep a card on file for automatic payment after each visit, or pay invoices online from your Jane account.

What is a superbill?

A superbill is a detailed receipt with the diagnosis and treatment codes your insurance company needs. You can download it from your Jane account and submit it for out-of-network reimbursement.

How do I reschedule or cancel?

Use the link in your Jane reminder email or sign in to your account. Please give us at least 24 hours' notice so we can offer the time to another family.

Is my information secure?

Yes. Jane is HIPAA-compliant and encrypts your health and payment information. Please don't send medical details through our website contact form — share them securely in your Jane intake forms instead.
