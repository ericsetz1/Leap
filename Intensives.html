Intensive Therapy

What is intensive therapy?

Intensives are short-term, high-frequency, multi-disciplinary pediatric therapy programs designed to provide focused intervention over a concentrated period of time. Intensive therapy allows for increased repetition of specific skills to boost neuroplasticity with the hope of meeting goals in a shorter amount of time!

Our intensive program may include specialized therapeutic approaches including:

  • Dynamic Movement Intervention (DMI)
  • Reflex integration
  • Sensory integration
  • NeuroMuscular Electrical Stimulation (NMES)
  • Vestibular-visual treatment
  • Traditional physical and occupational therapy practices

We also work closely with speech therapists who may be able to provide:

  • Speech therapy
  • Alternative Augmentative Communication (AAC)
  • Feeding therapy services during your intensive depending on availablity

Individualized Intensive Experiance

We believe every child and family is different, and we specialize in meeting children where they are, but seeing their potential and pushing them to achieve incredible milestones! Each child’s intensive will be individualized based on:

  • Primary therapy goals
  • Current functional abilities
  • Recommended disciplines
  • Treatment frequency
  • Child’s tolerance and endurance
  • Family priorities
  • Appropriate therapeutic approaches

At the conclusion of the intensive, each family will receive a home exercise program and training to confidently carry on programming at home!
