Our Team

Meet the therapists behind Leap

We started Leap Pediatric Therapy to create the kind of practice we'd want for our own families — unhurried, playful, and built on real partnership with parents.

Rachel Clouatre

PT · Co-Founder

Co-Founder & Pediatric Physical Therapist

Rachel earned her Doctor of Physical Therapy from Texas Woman’s University in Houston and has five years of experience in pediatric outpatient therapy. She holds an Introductory Level C certification in Dynamic Movement Intervention (DMI) and is passionate about continuing to grow her knowledge and skills to better serve the children she works with. Rachel is dedicated to supporting early childhood development through evidence-based care, purposeful movement, and play, helping each child build new skills and reach their unique potential.

Outside of the clinic, Rachel loves to spend time with her family. As a mom of two, Rachel brings both her professional experience and personal appreciation for the joys and challenges of childhood development. When she has downtime, she enjoys painting, reading a good book, and spending time outdoors.

Hannah Norman

OT· Co-Founder

Co-Founder & Pediatric Occupational Therapist

Hannah earned her Master of Occupational Therapy from the University of St. Augustine – Austin and has been practicing in outpatient pediatric settings for the past 5 years. She has completed continuing education in sensory integration, primitive reflexes, vestibular and oculomotor function, and developmental milestones. She has earned Introductory Level C certification in Dynamic Movement Intervention and has recently begun taking courses for the Masgutova Neurosensorimotor Reflex Integration (MNRI) Method.

In her spare time, Hannah enjoys many hobbies including jewelry making, painting, photography, and reading. She enjoys spending time on the water with her family in Mississippi on the weekends.
