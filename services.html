Services

How we help kids move, play, and grow

We treat children from birth through adolescence in our clinic. Not sure if your child would benefit from therapy? Ask us — we're happy to talk it through.

Infant & Early Childhood Development

  • Torticollis and head-shape concerns (plagiocephaly)
  • Tummy-time tolerance
  • Delayed rolling, sitting, crawling, or walking
  • Prematurity follow-up

Gross Motor Developmental

  • Balance and coordination challenges
  • Low muscle tone and weakness
  • Motor planning and clumsiness
  • Developmental coordination disorder

Fine Motor Development

  • Toe walking and in-toeing
  • Flat feet and orthotic recommendations
  • Post-surgical and post-casting rehab
  • Scoliosis and posture

Sensory Regulation & Processing

  • Sports injuries and return-to-play
  • Strength and conditioning
  • Injury prevention
  • Hypermobility

Reflex Integration

  • Cerebral palsy
  • Down syndrome and genetic conditions
  • Spina bifida
  • Autism spectrum disorder

Dynamic Movement Intervention

  • Personalized home exercise programs
  • Equipment and toy recommendations
  • School and daycare collaboration
  • Parent education sessions

Appointments we offer

Initial Evaluation

A comprehensive 60-minute assessment of your child's movement, strength, and milestones, with a written plan of care.

Weekly Treatment

One-on-one, play-based therapy sessions focused on your child's goals, typically 30–45 minutes.

Intensive Model of Therapy

typically 1-3 weeks for 2-4 hours/day.

Free Phone Consult

A quick 15-minute call to answer your questions and see whether physical therapy is a good fit.
