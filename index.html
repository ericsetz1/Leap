What we do

Therapy that feels like play

Every session is built around your child's goals and interests, so progress happens while they're having fun.

👶

Infant development

Support for torticollis, plagiocephaly, tummy-time struggles, and reaching milestones like rolling, sitting, crawling, and walking.

🧸

Gross motor skills

Building balance, coordination, strength, and endurance so kids can run, jump, climb, and keep up with their friends.

⚽

Sports & orthopedics

Injury recovery, toe walking, gait concerns, and post-surgical rehab for active kids and teens.

Your first visit, step by step

Reach out
